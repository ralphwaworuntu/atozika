## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a vibrant, delightful, and highly motivating modern language learning environment. Built for learners of all ages, it fuses intuitive productivity with tactile gamification. The visual language conveys warmth, positivity, and clarity—reducing cognitive fatigue while encouraging daily persistence through celebratory visual feedback.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Ed-Tech Tactility** with **Playful Minimalism**:
- **Playful & Friendly:** Expansive corner radii, inviting pill-shaped actions, soft pastel accents, and expressive gamification badges (streaks, gem counters, achievement ranks).
- **Crisp & Clean:** Floating white card containers set against gentle atmospheric sky tones create a light, organized canvas that frames rich 3D character illustrations and cultural scenes cleanly.
- **Supportive & Clear:** Generous whitespace, distinct typographic hierarchy, and unambiguous progress signifiers make daily language goals feel achievable and rewarding.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uplifting, optimistic tone. It utilizes high-vibrancy primary blues for key actions and progress indicators, balanced against soft sky canvas tones and warm accent hues for streaks and achievements.

### Surface & Atmosphere
- **Canvas Base:** `#F0F6FF` transitioning into `#F8FAFC` for high-depth atmospheric comfort.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` pure white cards with soft boundary blurs provide high-contrast legibility.
- **Subtle Surface Tints:** `#EFF6FF` for active navigation selections, banner underlays, and soft interactive pills.

### Primary, Accents & Gamification
- **Primary Interactive:** `#2563EB` (Cobalt Blue) with hover states anchoring at `#1D4ED8` and active states at `#3B82F6`.
- **Streak & Energy Accent:** `#F59E0B` (Amber Flame) and `#F97316` (Warm Tangerine) reserved for daily streaks, energy meters, and celebration states.
- **Gems & Currency Accent:** `#0EA5E9` (Vivid Sky Cyan) and `#38BDF8` for gem counters, token balances, and unlockable rewards.
- **Success & Skill Mastery:** `#10B981` (Emerald Mint) for completed exercises, lesson checkmarks, and vocabulary retention tiers.

### Soft Pastel Skill Accents
Pastel pill and container accents provide instant category distinction across learning modalities:
- **Listening:** Pastel Violet (`#EDE9FE` background, `#7C3AED` icon & text).
- **Speaking:** Pastel Coral (`#FFE4E6` background, `#E11D48` icon & text).
- **Vocabulary:** Pastel Mint (`#DCFCE7` background, `#16A34A` icon & text).
- **Grammar:** Pastel Amber (`#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` icon & text).

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded geometric apertures and clear humanist proportions convey warmth, accessibility, and modern polish. 

- **Display & Headings:** Set in heavy weights (Bold `700`, ExtraBold `800`) with tight negative letter tracking to give greetings, titles, and lesson goals an enthusiastic, punchy presence.
- **Gamification & Numerical Readouts:** Streaks, gem tallies, and XP scores utilize Bold `700` styling to stand out against UI chrome.
- **Instructional Body Copy:** Weighted between Medium (`500`) and Regular (`400`) to guarantee fatigue-free readability during immersive grammar drills and story comprehension.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform organizes content using a fluid, dashboard-style grid engineered for smooth multitasking and quick orientation across lesson modules.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** A three-column architecture consisting of a persistent 240px navigation rail on the left, an expandable central primary learning stage (fluid, min 640px), and a dedicated 320px gamification/social sidebar (Daily Goals, Leaderboards, Unlockable Quests) on the right.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** A two-column structure where the secondary gamification sidebar docks underneath the main stage or into slide-over panels, while the main navigation condenses to a compact icon-plus-label rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with top sticky stat bars (Streak, Gems, Notifications) and an ergonomic bottom navigation bar.

### Spacing Rhythm
Empty layout space relies on a cohesive 4px/8px incremental scale. Component gap spacing prioritizes breathing room around modular cards (`space-md` to `space-lg`), ensuring cards never feel cramped while maintaining clear contextual groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft ambient light cast onto a clean sky background, creating a tactile, pillowy card atmosphere rather than harsh structural borders.

- **Level 0 (Atmospheric Canvas):** Soft, faint gradient backdrop (`#F0F6FF` to `#F8FAFC`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card Surfaces & Floating Widgets):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card elements floating slightly above the canvas with ultra-soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Focus States):** Active hero containers and high-priority action cards: `box-shadow: 0 18px 32px -8px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10), 0 10px 14px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Modals, level-up celebrations, and floating menus: `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`.
- **Interactive Depth (Tactile Buttons):** Primary action buttons use a subtle bottom inset or lower offset shadow, giving them a physical, tap-friendly presence that depresses slightly on active click (`translate-y-[1px]`).

## Shapes

The shape system is friendly, soft, and organic:

- **Large Canvas Cards & Hero Modules:** Formed with large radii (24px to 28px / `rounded-3xl`), producing a welcoming, approachable aesthetic reminiscent of high-end tactile hardware and playful consumer apps.
- **Content Blocks & Skill Category Containers:** Shaped with 16px to 20px radii (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Interactive Controls & Status Indicators:** Standard buttons, input search fields, streak counters, and skill badges adopt full pill contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), ensuring high tactile affordance and thumb-friendly ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), saturated Cobalt Blue background (`#2563EB`), bold white text, smooth scale micro-interaction (`hover:scale-[1.02] active:scale-[0.98]`). Paired with a directional chevron or icon on completion/start flows.
- **Ghost & Secondary Buttons:** White surfaces with faint borders (`#E2E8F0`) or soft blue tints (`#EFF6FF`), offering gentle secondary pathways without competing with primary calls to action.
- **Upgrade/Special Action:** Distinct gradient fill (e.g., `#3B82F6` to `#1D4ED8`) or gold-accented badges to highlight premium value.

### Gamification & Stat Badges
- **Streak & Gem Counters:** Pill containers with pure white backgrounds, subtle border definition, flex-aligned with vibrant 3D or flat emojis/icons (Flame `#F59E0B`, Gem `#0EA5E9`) and crisp bold numeral counts.
- **Daily Goal Progress Ring:** Circular progress gauge rendered in smooth primary blue and subtle track tint, featuring centered goal metrics (e.g., "15 / 20 min") and contextual streak validation.
- **Weekly Consistency Dot Matrix:** Horizontal row of circular day nodes (Mon–Sun) with solid primary blue fills and white checkmarks for completed days, paired with hollow light-slate rings for upcoming days.

### Cards & Module Containers
- **Hero Welcome Banner:** Generous card housing a dynamic sky gradient (`#DDEBFF` to `#E0F2FE`), playful mascot character art, contextual greetings, and an immediate "Continue Learning" CTA.
- **Skill Practice Tiles:** Four-column modular cards featuring pastel tinted icon containers (Listening, Speaking, Vocabulary, Grammar) paired with small arrow indicator affordances to denote quick-launch practice paths.
- **Leaderboard Ranks:** Compact vertical list within a white card; items feature rounded rank medals (Gold `#F59E0B`, Silver `#94A3B8`, Bronze `#D97706`), avatar rings, bold usernames, and distinct XP totals. Active user row features a soft blue spotlight fill (`#EFF6FF`).

### Input Fields
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`) with a soft white or sky-tinted fill (`#F8FAFC`), interior search icon in muted slate (`#94A3B8`), and subtle border transition to primary blue upon focus.

### Progress Bars
- **Lesson Mastery Track:** Rounded pill tracks (`rounded-full`) filled with soft gray/blue track background (`#E2E8F0`) and smooth primary blue animated progress fills with percentage labels aligned neatly to the side.
